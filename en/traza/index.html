Traceability

Every object, with its identity and its full history.

With Traza, your documents, products, and assets have a verifiable trail: you know where they came from, where they've been, who moved them, and where they are now. Traza has its own website.

Go to traza.technology (opens in a new tab)Write to us
What it does

Identify, record, verify, and look up.

For distribution, retail, logistics, services, and document management companies that need to know where everything is and where it's been. QR codes, barcodes, RFID, and integrations, depending on what each case needs.

Unique identity

Every object has its origin and its own unique code.

Clear accountability

Every movement is recorded along with who made it.

Evidence

You verify the location and the evidence for every step.

Complete history

You see the whole history, physical and digital.
