Signing in

The supervisor sets up the phone once. The first time, you sign in with your username; after that, with your name and your PIN.

Setting up the phone (the supervisor does this)

A new phone opens on «Preparar teléfono» (“Set up phone”). If you find it like that, it isn’t yours to set up: the screen says «Llama al supervisor» (“Call the supervisor”).

  1. Connect it to the gate’s Wi-Fi.

    It can’t be set up without a connection: «Para registrar el teléfono necesitas conexión. Conéctate al Wi-Fi de la garita.» (“You need a connection to register the phone. Connect to the gate’s Wi-Fi.”)

  2. In Odoo, generate the enrollment code.

    It’s under Odoo › Seguridad › Configuración › Teléfonos › Generar código de alta (Odoo › Security › Configuration › Phones › Generate enrollment code). Odoo shows a QR code.

  3. Tap «Escanear QR» (Scan QR) and point at the QR code in Odoo.

    You don’t type the enrollment code: you scan it. Meanwhile the app says «Registrando…» (“Registering…”).

  4. Check «Teléfono listo» (“Phone ready”).

    Below it you see the post and the site the phone is now sealed to. If they’re right, tap Entrar (Sign in).

One phone, one site

The QR code seals the phone to one site and one post. Only guards assigned to that site can sign in. To move it to another site, it has to be set up again.

Your first time on this phone

  1. Tap «Otro usuario» (Another user).

    If nobody has used the phone yet, the app opens straight on «Tu usuario» (“Your username”).

  2. Enter your username and tap «Seguir» (Continue).

    It’s the short username the security administrator gave you. It isn’t your ID number or an email address.

  3. Enter your password and tap «Entrar» (Sign in).

    Ver (Show) lets you see what you typed. The password is only asked for this once on this phone. You need a connection.

  4. Create your PIN: six digits.

    The screen says «Crea tu PIN» (“Create your PIN”) and then «Repite tu PIN» (“Repeat your PIN”). You use that PIN to sign in and also to sign records.

If the PIN isn’t accepted, the app tells you why in two words:

  • «Muy fácil» (“Too easy”): don’t use consecutive digits (123456) or the same digit repeated (000000).
  • «No son iguales» (“They don’t match”): the second one doesn’t match the first. Enter it again.

If you see a message

MessageWhat to do
«Usuario o contraseña incorrectos.» (“Incorrect username or password.”)Check how you typed your username. If it keeps happening, talk to the security administrator.
«Tu usuario no está asignado a esta sede. Habla con el administrador.» (“Your user isn’t assigned to this site. Talk to the administrator.”)Your username exists, but not for this gate. The administrator fixes it.
«Para entrar por primera vez en este teléfono necesitas conexión…» (“To sign in for the first time on this phone you need a connection…”)It’s not your password: it’s the signal. Connect to the gate’s Wi-Fi and tap Entrar (Sign in) again.
«Tu contraseña es temporal y todavía no se puede cambiar en la app…» (“Your password is temporary and can’t be changed in the app yet…”)You can’t sign in with a temporary password. Talk to the security administrator.
«Esta cuenta es de administrador…» (“This is an administrator account…”)That account isn’t a guard account. It’s used on the administrator’s phone.

Every day

  1. On «Toca tu nombre» (“Tap your name”), tap your name.

    It shows the last people who signed in on this phone, with their initials.

  2. Enter your PIN.

    If you were left on duty, the app reminds you: «Sigues encendido desde las …» (“You’ve been on duty since …”) and you go straight to Hoy (Today). If not, it asks «¿Empiezas a trabajar?» (“Starting work?”). See Going on and off duty.

Tapped the wrong name? Tap No soy yo (Not me), above the keypad.

Several guards, one phone

The phone keeps up to 8 profiles and shows the 4 most recent. If your name isn’t there, tap Otro usuario (Another user) and sign in with your username and password, like the first time.

Everyone signs with their own PIN. Whatever you sign is recorded under your name, even though the phone is shared.

If you get your PIN wrong

  • Each mistake: «PIN incorrecto» (“Incorrect PIN”) and the dots shake.
  • Five mistakes in a row: the keypad makes you wait. A clock appears with «Espera hasta …» (“Wait until …”). The wait gets longer: 5, 15 and 60 minutes.
  • Before the last attempt: «PIN incorrecto. Si fallas otra vez, tendrás que entrar con tu contraseña.» (“Incorrect PIN. If you get it wrong again, you’ll have to sign in with your password.”)
  • Ten mistakes in total: «Borramos tu acceso en este teléfono por seguridad. Entra con tu usuario y tu contraseña.» (“For security, we removed your access on this phone. Sign in with your username and password.”) Your name is removed from the phone. Nothing happens to anyone else.

If you forgot your PIN

  1. Tap your name and then «Olvidé mi PIN» (I forgot my PIN).

    It’s above the keypad.

  2. Enter your password.

    You need a connection.

  3. Create a new PIN.

    Six digits, twice.

If you forgot your password

Tap Olvidé mi contraseña (I forgot my password). The app says: «Pide al administrador de Seguridad una temporal.» (“Ask the security administrator for a temporary one.”) Talk to them: the password can’t be changed from the phone.

A temporary password won’t get you into the app

If you enter a temporary password, the app won’t let you continue: «Tu contraseña es temporal y todavía no se puede cambiar en la app.» (“Your password is temporary and can’t be changed in the app yet.”) Ask the administrator how to make it permanent.

When it locks by itself

If nobody touches the phone for a few minutes, or the screen turns off, the app locks. When you come back you see «Se bloqueó por seguridad. Tu trabajo sigue donde lo dejaste.» (“Locked for security. Your work is right where you left it.”) Enter your PIN and you’re back on the same screen. The site sets how many minutes.

Making a call from the app doesn’t lock the phone. But if the call lasts longer than those minutes, it asks for your PIN when you come back.

Sometimes the app asks for more: «Por seguridad, entra otra vez con tu usuario y tu contraseña.» (“For security, sign in again with your username and password.”) Your session expired. Sign in with your password and create your PIN.

Signing out of this phone

Only do this if you’re no longer going to work at this gate. To go home, just go off duty.

  1. Tap «Más» (More) and then «Acerca de» (About).

    At the bottom is the Salir de este teléfono (Sign out of this phone) link.

  2. Read «¿Salir del teléfono?» (“Sign out of the phone?”).

    It says «Vuelves con contraseña» (“You’ll come back with your password”) and, if you have anything half-finished, how many checks or entries will be deleted.

  3. Tap «Sí, salir» (Yes, sign out).

    The app takes you off duty, sends whatever it can («Saliendo…», “Signing out…”) and removes your name from the phone. At the end: «Saliste de este teléfono.» (“You signed out of this phone.”)

Half-finished work is lost

A check or a paperwork-free entry you didn’t sign is deleted when you sign out. What you already signed isn’t: it waits on the phone until it’s sent.

If it asks you to update

When there’s a new version, the app lets you know: «Hay una versión nueva de Zentrix Check. Se descarga mientras trabajas.» (“There’s a new version of Zentrix Check. It downloads while you work.”) Tap Actualizar (Update) and keep working. When it says «La actualización está lista» (“The update is ready”), tap Instalar y reiniciar (Install and restart): the app closes for a few seconds and comes back on its own.

If the update is mandatory, you see «Actualiza Zentrix Check» (“Update Zentrix Check”) and it won’t let you continue: «Sin actualizar no puedes seguir trabajando.» (“You can’t keep working without updating.”) Tap Actualizar en Play (Update on Play). If Google Play doesn’t open, search the store for Zentrix Check.

If it says «Teléfono bloqueado» (“Phone blocked”)

Odoo revoked the phone or no longer recognizes it: «Este teléfono fue retirado de Zentrix Check.» (“This phone was removed from Zentrix Check.”) or «Odoo ya no reconoce este teléfono.» (“Odoo no longer recognizes this phone.”) It can’t be used again until it’s set up again. Call the supervisor.

If there are entries not yet sent, the screen says so and they aren’t deleted. Preparar de nuevo (Set up again) is for the supervisor only: it sets aside whatever wasn’t sent and asks for a new enrollment QR code.
