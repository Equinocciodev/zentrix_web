Infrastructure

Your apps and your data, always available.

Zentrix Cloud takes care of the hosting, monitoring and backups that keep your operation running, so your team can focus on the work.

Request a demoHow it works
The problem it solves

What changes for your team.

If an app goes down or data gets lost, your operation stops. Maintaining, monitoring and backing up servers takes time and people your team needs for other things.

Who it's for

For companies that want their apps and data available and backed up, without having to run the infrastructure themselves.

What you get

What Zentrix Cloud does for you.

  1. 01

    Hosting

    Your apps and your data, hosted.

  2. 02

    Monitoring

    We keep watch to make sure everything works.

  3. 03

    Backups

    Your information, backed up.

  4. 04

    Continuity

    Your operation keeps running.

Use cases

When it helps.

  1. 01

    Your apps need a home

    We host them along with their data, so your team can use them every day.

  2. 02

    You don't want to deal with servers

    Maintaining, monitoring and backing them up is on us.

  3. 03

    You can't afford to stop

    If something fails, your information is backed up and your operation keeps running.

How it works

Step by step, with you.

This is how we work at Zentrix: from understanding your operation to your team using it day to day, and we keep improving from there.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We look at how your team works today and what you need to solve.

  2. 02

    Design

    We fit the solution to your processes and your rules.

  3. 03

    Implement

    We set it up, integrate it with your systems and load your data.

  4. 04

    Adopt

    We stay with your team until using it is second nature.

  5. 05

    Improve

    We measure the results and keep improving with you.
