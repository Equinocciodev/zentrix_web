Zentrix Logistics · Logistics and delivery

From the warehouse to the door, without losing track.

Replenishment, picking, dispatch, delivery, cash on delivery and returns on top of your Odoo®, with your team working from Zentrix Work. It already moves GUUAO’s and Grupo Leiros’s orders every day.

Let’s talkSee the journey
The problem it solves

Between the warehouse and the door, things get lost.

The order leaves the system, but the parcels, the dispatch note, who carries it, what was collected and what came back end up in spreadsheets, paper and chat groups. When the customer asks, nobody can answer for sure.

Zentrix Logistics keeps everything in a single Odoo record and asks every step for its proof: the scan, the label, the pickup code, the photo and the receipt.

Who it is for

For retail, distribution and consumer goods companies that pick, dispatch and deliver every day, with Odoo® as their inventory system. If you don’t have it yet, Zentrix Software implements it.

How it works

Seven steps, one record.

Every step writes to Odoo as it happens and hands the next task to whoever has to do it.

  1. 01

    Replenish

    Odoo’s replenishment rules, kept up to date without slowing down sales.

  2. 02

    Pick

    The order rings on the phone, every item is scanned and every parcel gets its rack slot.

  3. 03

    Pack

    A picking ticket and a label per parcel, printed on their own at the store station.

  4. 04

    Dispatch

    A dispatch batch with driver, vehicle and dock, and a dispatch note that follows the invoice.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    With your own fleet or an external courier. Drivers order their stops and navigate by voice.

  6. 06

    Collect

    At the door, in cash or by card terminal, with a photo of the receipt and its reference.

  7. 07

    Return

    What wasn’t delivered comes back with a reason, and authorized returns are received in store.

Modules

Everything that happens between the shelf and the customer.

Every module says whether it is in production or in development. We don’t sell what doesn’t exist yet.

  • Delivery with your own fleet or a third party

    Your drivers work from Zentrix Work, and the external courier (ARMI today) is quoted by load, dispatched and tracked by webhook, all in the same flow.

    In productionHow drivers deliver

  • Replenishment

    Replenishment with Odoo’s rules, fast even with thousands of products, and stock that can’t go negative. Suggested minimums and maximums are on the way.

    In productionIn developmentWhat’s ready

  • Picking and packing

    Camera scanner, shortages with a suggested substitute, parcels on the rack, a label per parcel and FEFO at the point of sale: the lot that expires first leaves first.

    In productionHow picking works

  • Dispatch and dispatch notes

    Dispatch batches with driver, vehicle and dock. No parcel leaves until its label is scanned, and the dispatch note reaches the invoice. Automatic route notes are in development.

    In productionIn developmentWhat’s ready

  • Live tracking and control room

    Orders per store, the fleet map and incidents in one console. From there you can warn a customer about a delay or pause a store.

    In productionWhat’s ready

  • Cash on delivery

    Every payment has an owner and a clock: declared at the door, reconciled when the trip closes, and anyone holding cash for over 24 hours can’t start a shift without approval.

    In productionHow drivers collect

  • Returns

    Stores receive only what Support authorized, with a mandatory photo. On the road, “Couldn’t deliver” records the reason and the order comes back.

Integration

Three layers that work as one.

Zentrix Logistics isn’t another system to sync. It lives on top of your Odoo inventory and gives your people in store and on the road an app built for their job.

  1. The people

    Zentrix Work

    Pickers, counter staff, drivers and whoever receives returns work from their phone, with shifts, alerts that ring, a scanner and proof of delivery.

    Learn more Zentrix Work
  2. The rules and the road

    Zentrix Logistics

    Builds loads and dispatch notes, connects to couriers, asks for proof at every step and gives the control room a view of the street.

  3. The inventory

    Odoo®

    Products, lots and expiry dates, warehouses, replenishment, transfers, invoices and payments. The single source of truth.

    Learn more Odoo®
Industries

Built for companies that move product every day.

Retail and home delivery

Stores that pick online orders and hand them over at the counter or at the door.

  • Guided picking with a scanner and parcels on the rack.
  • In-store pickup with a code, and age checks for orders with liquor.
  • Delivery with your drivers or an external courier.

Wholesale distribution

Dispatch by batches and routes, with drivers, vehicles and invoices handed to the customer.

  • Dispatch batches with driver, vehicle and dock.
  • Dispatch notes that follow the invoice and the order.
  • Credit terms counted from delivery, not from the invoice.

Consumer goods

Products with lots and expiry dates, high turnover and cash collected on the road.

  • FEFO: the lot that expires first leaves first.
  • No negative stock and no made-up lots.
  • Cash on delivery with custody and reconciliation.

Transparency

What’s in production and what’s in development.

We’d rather be clear from the start.

In production In production today

  • Guided picking with a scanner, parcels on the rack, a ticket and a label per parcel, and print agents in each store.
  • FEFO and lot control at the point of sale. No negative stock.
  • Replenishment with Odoo’s rules, without slowing down sales.
  • Dispatch batches, dispatch notes through to the invoice, invoice hand-over and pickups scheduled with driver and vehicle.
  • Delivery with in-house drivers on Zentrix Work and an external courier (ARMI, prepaid).
  • Control room: orders per store, fleet map, incidents, delay alerts and store pausing.
  • Cash on delivery with custody, reconciliation and trip closing.
  • Authorized returns received in store.

In development In development

  • Suggested minimums and maximums, forecasts and purchase arrival dates.
  • Planned cycle counts, so no product goes more than 90 days uncounted.
  • Proposed lot swaps, applied only with approval.
  • Route dispatch notes generated as drafts.
  • Automatic pricing for your own fleet and vehicle choice by weight.
  • Cash on delivery through the external courier.

What it doesn’t do

It doesn’t optimize routes on its own: drivers order their stops. And, for now, there is no tracking page for the end customer.
