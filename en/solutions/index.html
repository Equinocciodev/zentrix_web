By industry

Built for how your industry works.

What we already run every day, in production.

Retail and stores

The order comes in, your store team picks and delivers it, and the till balances at closing.

  • Zentrix Work: alerts your store team, guides picking with the scanner and records the delivery.
  • Payments and digital commerce: point of sale, tills and payment gateways.
  • Odoo® in Venezuela: inventory, sales and treasury with Venezuelan localization.

Distribution and route sales

Your reps are out in the field, sometimes without signal, and the office needs to know what was sold and collected.

  • Zentrix Sales: orders, visits and collections even without signal, straight into your system.
  • Zentrix Logistics: dispatch, routes and deliveries in one place (coming soon).
  • Odoo® in Venezuela: the management system that receives orders and payments.
