Venezuelan Odoo localization

What it covers, module by module.

We run and maintain it in production on Odoo 19, at distribution and retail companies in Venezuela.

  1. 01

    SENIAT e-invoicing

    Odoo sends every invoice, credit note and debit note to Soluciones Láser, an authorized digital-invoicing provider, which assigns its control number.

  2. 02

    IGTF in Odoo

    The 3% IGTF tax is applied only to payments in foreign currency, in invoicing and at the point of sale.

  3. 03

    VAT and income-tax withholdings

    VAT (IVA) and income-tax (ISLR) withholding vouchers with a preview before they are issued, plus the municipal business tax.

  4. 04

    Purchase and sales books

    The legal purchase and sales books, with the VAT summary by rate for the tax return.

  5. 05

    Bolívars and dollars at the BCV rate

    Every document in both currencies at the BCV rate, with exchange differences and reports in bolívars and dollars.

  6. 06

    Fiscal printer at the point of sale

    Odoo Point of Sale prints on HKA and Bixolon fiscal printers, with IGTF on foreign-currency payments.

  7. 07

    Numbering and branches

    Invoice number, control number and delivery notes, with each document tied to its branch.

  8. 08

    Venezuelan payroll (LOTTT)

    Severance, vacations, profit sharing and settlements under the LOTTT labor law, the bank file and the ISLR ARC form.

Frequently asked questions

Odoo in Venezuela, plainly.

Does Odoo issue SENIAT digital invoices?

Yes. With the localization, Odoo sends every invoice, credit note and debit note to Soluciones Láser, an authorized digital-invoicing provider, which assigns the control number.

How does Odoo handle the IGTF tax?

Odoo applies the 3% IGTF only when the payment is in foreign currency, both on invoices and at the point of sale.

Can Odoo run in bolívars and dollars?

Yes. Every document is recorded in both currencies at the BCV rate, with exchange differences and reports in bolívars and dollars.

Does Odoo Point of Sale work with a fiscal printer?

Yes. It prints on HKA and Bixolon fiscal printers, with IGTF on foreign-currency payments.

Which Odoo version do you implement?

Odoo 19, the version our localization runs on in production today.
