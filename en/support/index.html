Zentrix Work

Store teams · Zentrix Work →

For almost everything, your first point of contact is your store manager: they set up your account, enable your tasks and can cut off access if you lose your phone. For anything else, write to us.

soporte@zentrix.company

Put Support — Zentrix Work in the subject line and tell us the email you use in the app, your store and what happened. If you can, add a screenshot.

Quick questions

I don’t have an account. How do I sign up?

There is no sign-up: the app is only for the staff of companies that use Zentrix Work, and your manager creates your account. If you are a customer of the store, what you are looking for is that company’s customer app.

I forgot my PIN

On the PIN screen, tap Forgot your PIN? You sign in again with your email and password and create a new PIN. You don’t lose your shift or any work you had in progress.

I forgot my password

It is the same password as your company account. Ask your manager to reset it, or write to us.

My orders don’t make a sound

Make sure you are clocked in at a store: when you are clocked out, the app never makes a sound. If you are clocked in, check that notifications for Zentrix Work are allowed and that the phone is not on “Do Not Disturb.” When you clock in, the app warns you if any of that is wrong.

It won’t let me clock in

The server checks that you are at the store, that you have permission there and that the task is enabled. The message on screen tells you which of those failed. If you think it is a mistake, tell your manager.

My shift ended on its own

This happens when the app has not heard from you for a long time: the phone has no signal or the app has not been used. It is on purpose, so your phone doesn’t keep ringing if you have left. Clock in again and carry on.

I lost my phone or got a new one

Let your manager know so they can close your access on the old phone. On the new one, install the app and sign in with your email and password. What the app stored on the old phone is not copied to the new one, on purpose.

I no longer work at the company

Your access is closed from the company’s system and the app stops working on your phone. You can uninstall it. To ask for your data to be deleted, see delete your account or your data.

Documentation

The operator documentation explains shifts, orders, picking, delivery and returns step by step.

Delete your account or your data — Zentrix Work

The app does not let you create accounts, so they are not deleted from it either: your company creates the account and your company closes it. To ask for this:

  1. Write to soporte@zentrix.company with the subject Data request — Zentrix Work, or tell your manager.
  2. Include your name and your account email, and whether you want to see, correct or delete your data.
  3. We respond within 30 business days at most.

What is deleted, what is kept because of a legal obligation and for how long is explained in the privacy policy, section 7. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete anything from the server.

What is kept because of a legal obligation, and for how long, is in clause 7 of the policy. Signing out does not delete data from the server.

Zentrix Check

The security gate · Zentrix Check →

For almost everything, your first point of contact is your company’s security administrator: they set up the gate phone, create your account and give you your password. For anything else, write to us.

soporte@zentrix.company

Put Support — Zentrix Check in the subject line and tell us your username, your site and what happened. Please don’t send us screenshots of records, plates, invoices or personal data.

Quick questions

I don’t have an account. How do I get in?

There is no sign-up: the app is only for the security staff of companies that use Zentrix Check. Your account is created by your company’s security administrator, for the site where you work.

How is the gate phone set up?

With a QR code the administrator generates in the company’s system. Once scanned, the phone is sealed to that site. Without that code the app does nothing.

I forgot my PIN

On the PIN screen, tap I forgot my PIN. You sign in again with your username and password and create a new PIN.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap I forgot my password: the security administrator gives you one. The password cannot be changed from the phone.

There is no signal at the gate

Keep working: what you do is stored encrypted on the phone and sent automatically when the signal returns, without duplicates. The app shows how many items are still waiting. The only thing that needs a signal is fetching what the system says about a new load.

The gate phone was lost or damaged

Tell the security administrator right away: they can revoke the phone, and the app wipes what it stored as soon as it reconnects. The new phone is set up with another QR code.

I no longer work at the company or at that site

Your access is closed from the company’s system. The records you signed are kept, because they are the log of what happened at the gate. To request access to or deletion of your personal data, see delete your account or your data.

Delete your account or your data — Zentrix Check

The app has no sign-up, so accounts aren’t deleted from it either: your company creates and closes them. To request access to, correction or deletion of your data:

  1. Write to soporte@zentrix.company with the subject Data request — Zentrix Check, or tell the security administrator.
  2. Include your name, your username and your site (or, if you don’t use the app, which gate and on what date your vehicle or load went through), and whether you want to see, correct or delete your data.
  3. We respond within 30 business days at most.

What is deleted, what is kept because of a legal obligation and for how long is in clause 7 of the privacy policy. Leaving the phone or uninstalling the app doesn’t delete anything from the server, and signed records cannot be changed: if something is wrong, it is corrected with a note.

Documentation

The guard documentation explains setting up the phone, a truck leaving, receipts, keys, assets and what to do without a signal step by step.
