Applied intelligence

Your company's knowledge, behind every decision.

Zentrix AI is your company's super-brain: it connects your company's knowledge and systems so you can look things up, analyze and act, always within defined permissions.

Request a demoHow it works
The problem it solves

What changes for your team.

Your company's information is scattered across systems, documents and people. When you need to make a decision, you have to hunt it down, ask for it and piece it together by hand. Zentrix AI connects it so it's there the moment you need it.

Who it's for

For companies that want their own information working for them every day: looking up data, analyzing results and running tasks on their systems, with control over who can do what.

What you get

What Zentrix AI does for you.

  1. 01

    APIs

    Access to and integration with your systems.

  2. 02

    Connectors

    Your data and your systems, brought together.

  3. 03

    AI agents

    Tasks carried out with context.

  4. 04

    Assistants

    Your company's knowledge, on hand.

  5. 05

    Role-based access

    Everyone sees and does only what their role allows.

  6. 06

    Action log

    There is a record of what was looked up and what was done, with human oversight where the process calls for it.

Use cases

When it helps.

  1. 01

  2. 02
