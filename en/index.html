Our products

Two apps for everyday work.

One for the team serving customers in the store, and one for the people selling in the field. Both are in production, real teams use them every day, and they connect to your management system.

The problem it solves

In a busy store, orders get lost in a flood of messages, nobody knows who's handling what, and personal phones keep ringing even after hours. Zentrix Work brings order to that work: every order reaches a specific person at the right moment, and there's a record of who did what.

Who it's for

For store teams who prepare orders, make deliveries, and take returns, and for managers who need to see the shift in real time. It runs on Android, on each person's own phone.

What you get

  • Work comes to you: the order rings in your pocket and you take it with a swipe.
  • Prepare orders without mistakes: scan each product with the camera.
  • Your phone gets a break: off shift, you don't get a single notification.
  • Managers see everything: who's on shift, what they're handling, and how long it takes.
Learn more about Zentrix Work

The problem it solves

Sales reps spend the day in the field, often with no signal, and orders arrive late, on paper or by text message. The office doesn't know what was sold or collected until the end of the day. Zentrix Sales sends each sale straight to your system, online or offline.

Who it's for

For distributors and field sales teams who visit customers, take orders, and collect on invoices, and for supervisors who need to follow the route. It runs on Android and iPhone.

What you get

  • Sell even with no signal: the order is saved and goes out on its own once you're back online.
  • Every visit is recorded: arrival, photos, signature, and wrap-up, step by step.
  • Collect against open invoices, with the day's exchange rate and a receipt.
  • Your ERP has the final say: prices and taxes are recalculated when the order arrives.
