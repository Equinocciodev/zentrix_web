Manual for version 5.3.0

The Zentrix Sales rep manual

What’s new in version 5.3.0. The app now carries the Zentrix Sales brand.

  • A new look: an icon with the Zentrix “z”, a light theme by default (the dark one is still in Perfil (Profile)), the Inter typeface and Zentrix blue on buttons and messages. If you had already picked a theme, it stays.
  • When you sign in, you type your company’s full domain. See Signing in and syncing.
  • Perfil › Acerca de (Profile › About) has the website, Soporte (Support) and the privacy policy. For questions about your personal data, write to soporte@zentrix.company.
  • The orders, payments and data saved on your phone stay as they are, and you don’t have to sign in again.

Some screenshots in this manual still show the previous design, but the steps are the same.

What you can’t forget

If you read nothing else today, read this. It’s five sentences, and each one saves you an argument with a customer.

Don’t get this wrong

«Enviada» (Sent) is not «Aprobada» (Approved). When you record a payment, tell the customer you have reported it — not that their invoice is paid. Someone in Finance reviews it later, and until they approve it, that debt is still open.

Don’t get this wrong

The price you see is a quote, not the invoice. The office recalculates prices and taxes when it receives the order, using the ones in effect the day it arrives. If the number changes, the app lets you know.

Don’t get this wrong

Nothing you do gets lost. Everything is saved on your phone the moment you do it and goes out by itself as soon as you get a signal: orders, collections, visits and returns. Even if you close the app, your phone shuts off or you spend the whole day without internet.

And the two habits:

  • Sync in the morning. Two minutes with a good signal and you can work all day, even if you lose internet later.
  • Close each visit the same day. An open visit hasn’t reached anyone. You’ll find them in Actividad › Pedidos › «Abiertos» (Activity › Orders › Open).

What Zentrix Sales does

With Zentrix Sales you run your whole day on the road from your phone: you arrive at the store, build the customer’s order, collect payment, record what they returned and leave proof that you were there. It replaces the notebook, the calculator and the call to the office to ask how much someone owes.

It’s built for the road, not the office. It works without internet.

The five tabs at the bottom

TabWhat it’s for
Inicio (Home)Your dashboard for the day. Exchange rates, what you’ve done so far today and the eight shortcuts.
Catálogo (Catalog)Everything you can sell, with prices and available stock.
Actividad (Activity)Your orders, your visits and your returns.
Cobranza (Collections)What customers owe you and the payments you’ve recorded.
Perfil (Profile)Your account, the color theme, permissions and the PIN.

You propose, the office confirms

Orders, collections and new customers all go through someone who reviews them. That isn’t red tape: it’s what protects you when there’s a complaint. The record shows what you sent, when and with what numbers.
