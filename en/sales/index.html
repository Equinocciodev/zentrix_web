The orders from the street, in your ERP the same day.

The app your field sales team uses to build its route, take the order, collect payment and leave proof of every visit. With a signal or without one.

The visit

Eight steps, only one of them required.

Arrival. The rest is recorded if it is needed, and the draft saves itself. The photo is not required: if the camera fails, the visit still closes. Check-in does not wait for GPS.

The order does not wait for the network.

It is saved on the phone and goes out by itself when the connection returns. Retrying never charges twice. Anything pending shows as “To send”, with its reason.

Your ERP has the last word.

What the customer signs travels as proof. Price, tax and total are recalculated by your system when the order arrives. With Odoo® 17, 18 and 19 the integration is native and in production; with the rest, built to fit your operation.

  1. Check-in Time and place of arrival.
  2. Stock count What is on the shelf.
  3. Order Customer price, stock and credit.
  4. Photos Evidence taken on the spot.
  5. Returns What comes back, with a reason.
  6. Notes Whatever does not fit in a field.
  7. Signature The customer's sign-off.
  8. Check-out The close, with its duration.
Security

That phone holds what every customer owes.

  • Encrypted local database, one per rep.
  • A PIN to open the app.
  • The session is your ERP's.
  • Crash diagnostics with no personal data.
