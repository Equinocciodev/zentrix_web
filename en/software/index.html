Your management system, shaped around how you work.

Zentrix Software builds our products, like Zentrix Work, and implements and customizes Odoo® for your company: from assessment to adoption.

The problem it solves

What changes for your team.

A management system only works if it reflects how your company really works, and if your team uses it. Installing it is the easy part. The hard part is configuring it, connecting it to what you already have, and getting people to adopt it.

Who it’s for

For distribution, retail, logistics, services, and document management companies that use Odoo® or want to start.

What you get

What Zentrix Software does for you.

Assessment

We understand your processes before we touch the system.

Configuration

Odoo® set up to fit your rules.

Integrations

Connected to your other systems and to our products.

Adoption

Your team using it day to day.

Use cases

When it helps.

  1. You’re starting with Odoo®We’re with you from assessment to adoption, without skipping over your processes.
  2. You already use it, but it doesn’t look like your companyWe configure it with your rules so it reflects how you work.
  3. You need to connect itWe integrate it with your other systems and with our products, like Zentrix Work and Zentrix Sales.
How it works

Step by step, with you.

This is how we work at Zentrix: from understanding your operation to your team using it every day, and we keep improving from there.

  1. Understand We see how your team works today and what you need to solve.
  2. Design We fit the solution to your processes and your rules.
  3. Implement We configure it, connect it to your systems, and load your data.
  4. Adopt We stay with your team until using it is second nature.
  5. Improve We measure the results and keep improving with you.
