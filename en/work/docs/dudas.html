Quick questions

The most common questions at the store, answered in two lines. If yours isn’t here, ask your manager.

Orders don’t ring on my phone

Is your shift on? When it’s off, it never rings. If it is on, check for an amber warning at the top or a red dot on the bell, and make a test call.

I forgot my PIN

Tap ¿Olvidaste tu PIN? (Forgot your PIN?) and sign in with your email. You won’t lose your shift or any work in progress. Step by step.

I forgot my password

Ask your manager to reset it. If you know it and want to change it: Tu perfil › Cambiar mi contraseña (Your profile › Change my password).

What do I enter under «Servidor» (Server)?

The full domain your manager gave you. Just the domain, with nothing after it. You type it once per phone.

A task doesn’t show up

You haven’t been authorized for it. Tell your manager: it’s fixed in the system, not on your phone.

My shift turned off by itself

Your phone went a long time without sending a signal, or the store closed. See the two clocks. Start your shift again and carry on.

I’m on and nothing comes in

There may be no work: «Nada pendiente. Cuando entre un pedido, te suena.» (“Nothing pending. When an order comes in, it’ll ring.”) If you see «Sin conexión: el trabajo nuevo no te llega» (“No connection: new work isn’t reaching you”), find a signal. If you’re on a break, tap Volver ya (Back now).

I’m going to eat. Should I end my shift?

No: take a 5, 10 or 15-minute break. Work you’ve already started stays yours.

Should I sign out at the end of the day?

No. Just end your shift. Signing out removes your account from the phone and asks for your email next time.

I get a message and it won’t let me continue

Read it: the system writes it for you and says what’s missing. The app can’t skip it. If you think it’s a mistake, tell your manager.

The top says «PRUEBAS» (TEST)

You’re on a test server, not in the real store. Tell your manager.

Does it drain my battery?

As little as possible. The screen stays on only while a task is open, and when your shift is off it does nothing. The dark theme uses less.

I lost my phone or got a new one

Tell your manager. On the new phone, install the app and sign in with the server, your email and your password.
