Operator manual

What’s new in version 1.1.7. The app is now called Zentrix Work and has a new look: light theme by default, the Inter typeface and a new icon.

  • There’s only one promised time, the system’s: the list, the details and «Es tuya» (It’s yours) all say the same thing, in the format «8:19 p. m.». See Picking an order.
  • The 15-minute break says 15, not 16, and below «Desliza para apagar» (Swipe to end) it now says «Al apagarte, tu teléfono deja de sonar.» (“When you go off, your phone stops ringing.”) See Your shift.
  • If you start your shift on another phone, the first one shuts off right away, on any screen.
  • If the rider already took the order, «Entregar» (Hand over) no longer appears. See Handing over in store.
  • The «No puedo» (I can’t) reasons depend on the type of call. See Calls.
  • «Cómo llegar» (Directions) no longer closes the app. See Home delivery.
  • Plurals spelled correctly: «1 bulto» (“1 package”), «1 Unidad» (“1 Unit”).

Some screenshots in this manual still show the previous design, but the steps are the same.

What is Zentrix Work

It’s the app you use to handle your store’s work from your phone. It’s not the shopping app: that’s your company’s customer app. You can have both installed at the same time.

With it:

  • You start your shift at a store and choose what you’ll handle.
  • A call rings when there’s work for you.
  • You pick orders, hand them over in store or deliver them to the door, and receive returns.

It’s your phone

The app runs on your personal phone. That’s why it looks after your battery, your data and your privacy:

  • Off means silent. With no shift on, you don’t get a single alert.
  • Location only during your shift. When you start, when you end and while you work. Never outside of it.
  • The screen stays on only while a task is open, not for your whole shift.
  • Your PIN never leaves your phone. It’s a lock that lives only there.

Who decides what you see

Your store manager gives you your account and authorizes your stores and tasks. The system is in charge: if something won’t let you through, the app tells you why with the server’s message, word for word. It’s not a problem with your phone.

Authorized is not switched on

For a task to ring for you, two things are needed: your manager has to authorize it, and you have to switch it on in today’s shift.
