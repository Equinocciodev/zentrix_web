Research

New ideas, tested before they reach your operation.

At Zentrix Labs, we research and validate new products: we build prototypes and test them before turning them into tools for your team.

The problem it solves

What changes for your team.

Some of your operation’s needs aren’t solved by any tool yet. Launching something new without testing it is risky. At Zentrix Labs, we research it, build a prototype, and validate it first.

Who it’s for

For companies with a challenge that doesn’t fit an existing product, and who want to explore it with us.

How it works

Step by step, with you.

This is how we work at Zentrix: from understanding your operation to your team using it every day, and we keep improving from there.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We see how your team works today and what you need to solve.

  2. 02

    Design

    We fit the solution to your processes and your rules.

  3. 03

    Implement

    We configure it, connect it to your systems, and load your data.

  4. 04

    Adopt

    We stay with your team until using it is second nature.

  5. 05

    Improve

    We measure the results and keep improving with you.
